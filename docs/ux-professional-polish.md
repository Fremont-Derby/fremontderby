- Antialiased type; slightly tighter heading tracking
- Softened extreme `font-weight: 900/950` on controls toward 700

- Selects/buttons: short color transitions; disabled selects quieter
- Status pills: no forced all-caps; weight 700
- Mobile: slightly tighter H1 and card radii
